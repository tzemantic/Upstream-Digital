FAQ

Common questions

How much does it cost?

It depends on what your business needs. After a free consultation, we’ll give you a clear quote. Because we work month to month, you’re never locked in.

How much should I budget for ads?

It depends on your business and your goals. Monthly ad budgets can be anywhere from $500 to several thousand dollars, and we’ll tailor the campaigns to fit.

How is ad spend billed?

Google, Meta and other ad platforms charge your own payment method directly. You pay us separately for our services, with one invoice at the end of each month. Your reports show exactly what was spent, so you always know where your budget stands. One thing to know: the platforms can spend slightly more or less than the monthly budget we set, because ad prices are decided in live auctions.

Do you require a long-term contract?

No. We work month to month, and you can cancel at the end of any month. There are no strict contracts, so if you’re not happy, you’re free to leave.

Who owns my website and ad accounts?

You do. Everything we build for you is yours, including your website and your ad accounts. We may set up your ad accounts for you, but they belong to you and stay with you if you ever leave.

How often will I hear from you?

You’ll get a monthly report and a weekly check-in. We can adjust that to fit your needs and schedule.

How soon will I see results?

Paid ads can start bringing in leads soon after they launch. SEO takes longer and builds over months. We’ll be upfront about what to expect and keep you updated along the way.

How do you measure success?

By what matters to your business, like qualified leads, cost per lead and new customers, not just clicks or impressions.

Can you take over from my current agency?

Yes. We’ll start by reviewing what’s already in place, keep what’s working and fix what isn’t. Spade Security came to us from another agency, and their cost per qualified lead dropped 96%.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

All kinds, from family-owned local businesses to companies that sell nationwide. Our clients span commodities, commercial security and entrance control, building supply, home improvement, restaurants, fitness and wellness, e-commerce and consumer brands, and government services.

Where is Upstream Digital located?

We’re based in Greenwich, Connecticut. We work with businesses across Connecticut and New York, and with clients elsewhere in the US.

What is AEO?

AEO, or answer engine optimization, helps your business show up in answers from AI assistants and search features like Google’s AI Overviews, not just in the regular list of search results. It builds on good SEO: clear, well-structured content that answers the questions your customers ask.
