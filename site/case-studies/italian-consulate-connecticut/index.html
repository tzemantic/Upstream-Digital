The client

The Honorary Consulate of Italy in Hartford helps Italian citizens and residents across Connecticut with passports, citizenship, notary services and the Italian tax ID code. Visits are by appointment only.

The challenge

Consular services come with a lot of requirements, and people often aren’t sure which office handles what. The consulate needed one clear place that explained each service and how to book, and it needed to show up when people searched for it.

What we did

New website

We designed and built a new website with its own page for passports, citizenship, notary services and the Italian tax ID code. Each page lays out what you need and how to book.

Clear next steps

Every service page explains how appointments work, and the contact page has a simple form, phone numbers and directions.

Easy to keep up to date

A news section makes it easy to post updates like voting notices and citizenship guidance.

Google Business Profile and local SEO

We improved the consulate’s Google Business Profile and helped the office keep the reviews coming, so it’s the first result when people in Connecticut search for it.

The results

The consulate is now #1 on Google and has more than 100 Google reviews. People can find the right service, see what they need and book an appointment without having to call first.
