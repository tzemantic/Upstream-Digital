The client

Phibro RenewOil picks up used cooking oil from restaurants, commercial kitchens, food trucks, hotels and other food businesses across Connecticut and parts of New York and New Jersey. They provide secure containers, come by on a regular schedule, and pay for every gallon they collect. The oil gets recycled into things like biodiesel.

The challenge

Most restaurant owners have no idea their used cooking oil is worth anything, so the offer has to be explained before anyone’s interested.

When we started, nearly all of Phibro’s new customers came from paid ads, and they barely showed up on Google. If the ad budget went down, so did new business.

What we did

Facebook and Instagram ads

We built Meta campaigns around the simplest selling point: you get paid for your used oil. We kept testing new images and videos and put more money behind whatever worked. We also ran ads in Spanish to reach Spanish-speaking restaurant owners.

Google Ads

We ran Google Ads alongside Meta to catch restaurants already searching for oil pickup. As Phibro started showing up on Google on its own, we moved the ad budget over to Meta.

Website

We redesigned the site around the businesses Phibro works with, from restaurants and food trucks to hotels and towns, and made the payment and pickup details easy to find. Getting in touch takes one short form.

Local SEO and AEO

We built more than 110 pages for the towns Phibro serves across Connecticut and New York, keep their Google Business Profile up to date, and check how things are going every month. Leads now come in from ads, the contact form, phone calls and their Google Business Profile.

The results

Today, Phibro’s ads, website and Google search all bring in leads, and those leads turn into new customers month after month. Most of their new business now comes in online.

And because more people find them through search, new business doesn’t hinge on the ad budget anymore.
