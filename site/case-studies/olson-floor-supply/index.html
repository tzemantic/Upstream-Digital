The client

Olson Floor Supply is a family-owned business that sells hardwood flooring, supplies, tools, machines and Benjamin Moore paint. Contractors, developers and homeowners across New York City, Long Island, Westchester and nearby Connecticut have been buying from them for more than 20 years.

The challenge

Olson’s business was built on word of mouth and customers who keep coming back. They needed a website that showed everything they sell, made it easy to send in orders ahead of pickup, and helped new customers in the area find them on Google.

What we did

Built from scratch

We went through every product detail with the owner, so the site is genuinely useful to customers. Everything is organized into flooring, Benjamin Moore paint, supplies and machines.

Built around how people buy

There are sections for contractors, developers and homeowners, a list of authorized dealers, customer reviews, and a spot where the team posts updates like the next machine service pickup date. A simple form lets customers send in orders ahead of time so they’re ready when they arrive.

Local SEO

We created 32 local pages, one for each product line in Brooklyn, Queens, Manhattan, the Bronx, Staten Island, Nassau County, Suffolk County and Westchester County.

Ongoing SEO

We keep working on Olson’s Google rankings so more people can find the store.

The results

Olson now has a website that shows everything they carry, plus local pages for all eight areas they serve. In the owner’s words, the finished site exceeded his expectations.

Questions about this project

What did Upstream Digital build for Olson Floor Supply?

A new website built from scratch, with catalogs for flooring, Benjamin Moore paint, supplies and machines, plus 32 local pages and ongoing SEO.
