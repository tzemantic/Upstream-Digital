Common questions

Which ad platforms do you manage?

Meta (Facebook and Instagram), Google and LinkedIn.

Do I need to advertise on every platform?

No. We recommend the platforms where your customers are and that fit your budget, and focus spend there.

How do you measure whether ads are working?

By the leads they bring in and what each qualified lead costs, not just clicks or impressions. We set up conversion tracking so those numbers are clear.

How much should I budget for ads?

It depends on your business and your goals. Monthly ad budgets can be anywhere from $500 to several thousand dollars, and we’ll tailor the campaigns to fit.

How is ad spend billed?

Google, Meta and other ad platforms charge your own payment method directly. You pay us separately for our services, with one invoice at the end of each month. Your reports show exactly what was spent. The platforms can spend slightly more or less than the monthly budget we set, because ad prices are decided in live auctions.

Who owns my ad accounts?

You do. We may set up your ad accounts for you, but they belong to you and stay with you if you ever leave.
