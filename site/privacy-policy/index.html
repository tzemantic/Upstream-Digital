Information We Collect

Information You Provide

If you contact us through a form, email, or other method, we may collect information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Company name
  • Any information you include in your message

We use this information only to respond to your inquiry or provide requested services.

Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, certain information may be collected automatically, including:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • Pages visited
  • Time spent on pages
  • Referring website

This information helps us understand how visitors use our website and allows us to improve user experience.

Use of Analytics and Tracking Technologies

We use tools such as Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager to better understand website traffic and user behavior.

These tools may use cookies or similar technologies to collect anonymous information about how visitors interact with our website.

The information collected may include:

  • Pages visited
  • Time spent on the site
  • Traffic sources
  • General geographic location

This data helps us improve our website performance and user experience. We do not use this information to personally identify visitors.

You can learn more about how Google uses information here: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Cookies

Our website may use cookies to improve functionality and analyze traffic.

Cookies are small files stored on your device that help websites remember information about your visit.

You can choose to disable cookies through your browser settings. Please note that some parts of the website may not function properly if cookies are disabled.

How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to inquiries or requests

  • Improve website functionality and performance
  • Analyze website traffic and usage trends
  • Maintain website security

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

Third-Party Services

Our website may use trusted third-party services to help operate the website or analyze traffic, including:

These services may collect information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information collected through our website. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Our website may contain links to other websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external sites.

Changes to This Privacy Policy
