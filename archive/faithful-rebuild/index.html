A practical approach to digital marketing

Upstream Digital was created to help businesses navigate digital marketing without all the unnecessary complexity. Too often, agencies throw the entire kitchen sink at a problem, recommending every service possible whether it’s needed or not.

We take a different approach. Our goal is to understand your business first and focus on the strategies that will actually make an impact. Sometimes that means running targeted ad campaigns. Other times it means improving your website or helping you rank higher on Google. Whatever the case, we focus on what you truly need, not what we can sell.

The result is a simpler, more focused approach to growing your business online.

How it works

Our Process

Step #1

Free Consultation

We start with a quick, no obligation call to learn about your business, your goals, and what you’re currently doing from a marketing standpoint. Whether you’re running ads, relying on referrals, or just getting started, we’ll walk through it together. We’ll also give you honest feedback on what we’re seeing and where there may be opportunities to improve.

Step #2

Strategy & Setup

After the call, we put together a clear, focused plan based on what your business actually needs. We don’t throw a bunch of services at you. If paid ads make the most sense, we’ll build and set up campaigns the right way. If your website or tracking needs work, we’ll take care of that too. Everything is built with lead generation and long-term growth in mind.

Step #3

Launch & Ongoing Optimization

Once everything is live, we actively monitor performance and continue improving over time. We’re not a set-it-and-forget-it agency. We make adjustments based on real data, test new ideas, and stay in communication with you so everything stays aligned with your goals.
